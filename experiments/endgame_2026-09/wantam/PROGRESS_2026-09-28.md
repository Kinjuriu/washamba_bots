What the planner does (wantam.py, class WantamController): simulates every product's
shared market to day 29 with the engine's exact price curves, both boards' supply and
the town + shop drain (plus expected future unlocks); gives each free tile to the crop
with the best marginal revenue per tile-day; lets an animal go (stops feeding) only when
its output under an optimistic demand forecast is worth less than feed + labour, and
commits to it (escapes all intended; unintended = 0 in every gate run).
