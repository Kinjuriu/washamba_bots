# W3 front-run: W3's tape with predicted-dump front-running (Builder A, 25 Sept)

`agents/w3_frontrun.py` is `agents/w3_herdsafe2700.py` byte-verbatim plus a market-only overlay. It is generated by `experiments/splice/_make_w3_frontrun.py [K]`, and `tests/test_w3_frontrun.py` has 9 tests.

## Summary

**It passes every ship criterion on both seed sets, with K = 6** (a predicted dump within 6 steps). It keeps W3's record against every opponent, adds wins where W3 dropped games, and wins the W3 mirror:

| | dev (900-915) | holdout (1000-1015) |
|---|---|---|
| vs W3 (mirror; need ≥ 20 of 32) | **32-0** | **26-6** |
| vs W1 (W3's own record) | 30-2 (26-6) | 20-12 (20-12) |
| vs W0 | 32-0 (24-8) | 28-4 (26-6) |
| vs reactive v7 | 32-0 (32-0), identical games | 32-0 (32-0), identical games |
| vs 2945 Farm | 32-0 (32-0) | 28-4 (28-4) |
| self-play mean vs W3's (need ≥ -300) | 104,176 vs 104,348 (-172) | 85,658 vs 85,779 (-121) |
| worst turn | 245 ms, first call including file load about 232 ms; the overlay itself adds ≤ 8 ms | same |

K = 12 is also a pass on dev but weaker everywhere. K = 24 loses the mirror edge (16-16) and does worse than W3 against the 2945 Farm. Selling just before the dump is the edge; selling a day early gives it back.

## 1. What the overlay does

The entrypoint is captured as W3's **last callable** right after the base text: `herdsafe_forecast_agent`, not W3's inner `agent` layer. Then every turn:

1. Feed `WB_DumpPredictor` (`experiments/splice/dump_predictor.py`, embedded) the observation, from step 0. It identifies the opponent from its money at steps 1-2 (W3 2,854 / W0 2,850 / 2945 Farm 2,860 / top six 2,460).
2. Call W3 and keep its action. Unit actions are returned untouched.
3. If a tape family is identified and predicts a dump of P within K steps, and W3's projected shed (after this turn's unit actions) holds P beyond what W3's own orders sell this turn, **add** `SELL P q` at the front of the market list:
   - q = the units whose quote now beats the price right after the dump, net of the drain in between;
   - P is one of MELON, STRAWBERRY, MILK, WOOL, TOMATO, CARROT, never WHEAT or FERTILIZER;
   - never more than 10 orders in total; if W3 already uses 10, nothing is added;
   - W3's orders are never removed or reordered; they follow the added ones, in their order.
4. `WB_FRONTRUN_ON = False` at the top of the overlay makes every action W3's own. The test replays a 240-step episode and compares every action; both flag-off seats of the seed-900 mirror bank W3's 70,559 exactly.

The predictor's dump table was regenerated to start at step 96 (it was 180 for the splice), so it now covers W3's pre-handover dumps: wool at 149/150, milk at 196. Held-out accuracy is unchanged: precision 0.74-0.75, recall 0.68-0.69.

## 2. What W3 already races, and why the overlay cannot double-advance it

W3 is already an aggressive seller, in four layers read from the source:

- **`_r36_reserve`** (steps 192-695) is W3 front-running its *own* tape. Every turn, for each product it is not already selling, buying or picking up, it sells now whatever its tape plans to sell within the race horizon, up to its projected stock. The horizon is `V9_RACE_DEFAULT` = 44 steps, up to `V9_RACE_MAX` = 48.
- **The V9 race** grows that horizon when it infers from the market that the rival sold while W3 still held stock, and a *racegate* variant handles glutted items (price at or below base + margin).
- **`_RACE_STATE` mirror gate**: at step 1 it detects a copy of itself (equal cash after turn 1) and switches to a mirror horizon ("against a copy the sale race is won only by pre-empting the whole day").
- **`_v92_predict`** (steps 150-699): forecasts the rival's MILK/WOOL/STRAWBERRY sales from its recent sale history and inserts SELLs at slot 0 (horizon 48).
- **`_v224_sales_first`** moves SELL orders ahead of buys in W3's list.

So W3 sells most of its stock within about 44 steps of the tape's plan. The overlay only ever adds units that W3's final orders are **not** selling this turn: projected shed minus W3's SELL quantity per product. The same units cannot be sold twice. What is left for it:

- stock W3 holds for tape sales beyond its race horizon;
- items W3 blocks this turn (a PICKUP or BUY of the item);
- pre-192 dumps, before `_r36_reserve` starts;
- turns where `_r36_reserve` abstains.

In practice that is 7-17 added orders per game, mostly milk and strawberry lots of 1-13 units on steps 350-700. Seed 900: 7 orders / 22 units; seed 901: 17 orders.

Notes:

- **Reactive v7 games are identical** because its opening matches no tape signature, so the predictor never fires.
- **Against the 2945 Farm** the mean edge is small (+190 dev, +19 holdout) and more individual games move down than up. Win counts are unchanged on both sets, but this is the weakest opponent for the overlay.
- **Timing.** A timing wrapper split each turn into W3's own time and the overlay's added time. The overlay adds at most 7.9 ms (mean 0.15 ms). Every worst turn is W3's own computation (its step-150 forecast or a step-503 layer): 132-245 ms on a quiet machine, and up to 459 ms while four gate workers and two other builders were running. One 568 ms reading came from W3's own step-150 turn under that load. W3 alone shows the same spikes (128-151 ms quiet).
- **Mirror.** Both W3 copies play the same tape, so they drop the same goods at the same steps. The overlay's handful of early sales, taken just before the other copy's dump, is the whole margin.
