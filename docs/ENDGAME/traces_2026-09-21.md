## 0. Method, and the two excluded episodes

The 19 reachable `56269928` episodes are the 4 verified `farm2945_losses` + 2
`farm2945_wins` + 10 name-matched rows in `experiments/endgame/panel/index.json`, plus
`109766905`, `109666520`, `109727399` cached in `experiments/endgame/rep/`.

**`109551974` and `109793226` are excluded from every table below** — they are the two
name-matched episodes where `agents/public_farm2945.py` does not reproduce the recorded
bank (`late_overlay_results.md` §1: −212 and +248, a `SELL MILK` / `SELL WOOL` quantity
drifting, which reads like a different submission version of the same agent). **n = 17,
15 losses and 2 wins, mean margin −7,498; losses only, −9,266.**

Unit counts here are **engine-exact, not parsed from order quantities**. Each episode was
re-run from *both* recorded tapes with `_commit_unit` and `_do_hire` instrumented, so every
committed market unit and every landed hire is logged with its seat and its price. **All 19
reproduce both banks to the dollar this way**, so the seat/seed conventions are right on
all of them; the exclusion above is about which *agent* played the seat, not about the tape.

The v219 counterfactuals in §1 load `agents/public_farm2945.py` as a module and seat it as
a live callable against the frozen opponent tape, monkey-patching `_v219_qualifies` in
memory. The file on disk is untouched. Control: the unpatched callable reproduces the
recorded bank to **$0 on all 4 episodes it was run on**.

> **Frozen-tape caveat, stated once.** In §1's counterfactual the opponent replays a
> recorded schedule it would not have played against a changed us. Read the *direction* and
> the *win count*, not the magnitude.

Bank better on **2 of 6**. Margin better on 4 of 6 but driven entirely by one seed

episodes are exact no-ops — blocked by price ($69, $62, $66, $68, $69, $62, and two more)
or by an already-unlocked SE quadrant.

And the shop draw does **not** predict the sign: the best outcome (`109835653`, +4,981) and
the worst (`109766905`, −6,524) both had exactly 2 PZ+FM instances.

### Verdict 1 — trap

Not "dependent on the shop draw": the shop draw does not separate the winners from the
losers, and the program itself loses where it already fires. It is a **trap**, with the
number being **−2,480 mean bank / −1,867 mean margin, 3 of 3, on the episodes where it
currently runs**, and **−1,307 mean bank, 2 of 6, zero flips** on the six the relaxed gate
newly reaches.

The tomato *book* is real — it is the one book farm2945 ends above base on, and the
opponent's 87 units a season are worth $6,959. What is wrong is v219's *price* of reaching
it: **$4,000 of land plus $3,300–3,800 of the day's most expensive hires**, for ten tiles
that yield 80 units. Any future tomato attempt has to reach the book without buying the
fourth quadrant and without hiring the 12th–14th hand of the day.

## 4. The "extra hand" repair layer: the lever is not there

Per the brief's own instruction — the traces do not support it, so this is the statement
instead of the sketch. Four independent reasons, each measured above:

1. **Price.** One hand every morning from day 12 costs **$2,872 a season** (§3.2), against a
   mean margin gap of **−7,498** (n=17) / **−9,266** (losses only). It would have to clear
   $2,872 of *new* net revenue before it broke even. **CLAUDE.md's "a hand is $1–$3, so any
   cash gate in front of hiring is mispriced" does not transfer to this base** — that was
   measured on our own agent at 4–6 hands/day. At 11 hands/day the fibonacci curve has
   turned the marginal hand into one of the most expensive purchases in the game.
2. **No work.** `PASS` is 5.0% of unit-turns, weeds 0.2–0.4 tiles, watering share identical
   to the opponent's (§3.3). "Waters unwatered plants, digs weeds" describes a job that
   does not exist on this farm.
3. **The structures clause is the wrong book.** "Builds and stocks up to 3 more structures"
   sells into WOOL and MILK. farm2945 already ends those books at **31% and 36% of base**,
   already outsells the 3,000+ opponent on both, and already finishes with **17.2 animals
   against their 11.9** (§2.3, §2.4). The marginal 30 WOOL units are worth **$47 each**.
   Three more animals ≈ 30–60 units ≈ $1,400–2,700 gross, against $900–1,500 of animal cost
   plus their share of the $2,872 — before the extra feed the crew has to carry.
   **The precondition the brief set — "a herd lever only exists if the extra animals'
   product goes into a book farm2945 is not already flooring" — fails for WOOL and MILK.
   It passes only for EGG (+$2,601, 103% of base), and that is a goose-count question
   inside the existing 17–18 structures, not a reason to hire a hand** (§2.4).
4. **It has already been paid for and measured.** `_v219_request` *is* an extra-hand layer:
   it hires 21–22 additional hands over days 18–29 and gives them their own targets and
   their own crop. It costs $3,300–$3,800 in hire fees and it loses on 3 of 3 (§1.2). The
   proposed layer is the same purchase with a worse product.

**The tile-collision risk the brief asked to be stated is real and is not the binding
problem.** A tape `PLANT` on a tile a hand has built on no-ops silently (`_apply_unit_action`
returns early when `tile is not None`), and worse, the engine drops **all** `PLANT X`
requests for a crop when a turn's demand exceeds held seed, so one stolen tile can void a
whole turn's planting for that crop. The clean tiles are the SE quadrant — which is
`LOCKED` and costs **$4,000**, i.e. the same purchase v219 makes. It is worth recording,
but the layer fails on price and on product before it gets to collisions.

**There is one further constraint any such layer must respect, found while reading the
base.** Hand indices are hire-order, and `_v219_request` refuses to fire unless
`len(farm['hands']) + parent_hires == expected` where `expected` is the max hand count in
the route's own day. An extra hand hired *before* the route's last daily `HIRE` shifts every
tape `hands[i]` by one **and** silently disqualifies v219 on the 3 episodes where it runs.
Any extra hire must go after the day's last tape `HIRE` and at the end of the market list
(orders settle in index lockstep, so prepending moves prices), under the 10-order cap.
