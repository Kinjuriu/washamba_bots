## Technical gotchas

- Kaggle runs the LAST callable in the file. When wrapping W3, capture its entry point with `[v for v in list(globals().values()) if callable(v)][-1]` (its name `agent` is an inner layer). Check with kaggle_environments.agent.get_last_callable.
- Replay convention: the action at steps[t] was chosen from steps[t-1]'s observation; the seed is in info.seed; replays re-simulate exactly.
- The market is per-unit lockstep by order slot; shops drain at step % 4 == 0, the town at step % 24 == 0; sales at the $1 floor add no inventory.
